# Code of Conduct

Enrama welcomes participants of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Contributors, maintainers, and people asking for help should be treated with
respect in issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and clear.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect different perspectives and accessibility needs.
- Accept reasonable feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and other people's time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation,
and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable. Publishing someone's
private information without consent is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

The maintainer, [@Dev-Peter21](https://github.com/Dev-Peter21), handles conduct
concerns. For a public interaction, you may flag the relevant issue or PR for
maintainer attention. If a report contains sensitive information, request a
private contact channel without posting the details publicly. No dedicated
private conduct-reporting address has been designated yet.

For abuse that cannot be safely raised in the project, use
[GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, request a change in behavior,
issue a warning, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently,
depending on severity and repetition. Reports should be handled discreetly;
information is shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

This policy applies to project spaces and when someone represents Enrama in
public. It is a project-specific policy, not an adoption of a third-party code.
